  <h2>3. Information Stored on Your Device</h2>
  <p>All data you create in the App &mdash; including tasks, notes, priorities, Brain Dump entries, and settings &mdash; is stored locally on your device using SQLite, a local encrypted database. This data never leaves your device except as described in Section 4 (Cloud Backup) and Section 6 (Third-Party Services).</p>
  <p>The following settings are stored locally on your device:</p>
  <ul>
    <li>Display name (first name used for personalized greetings)</li>
    <li>Week start day preference</li>
    <li>Date format preference</li>
    <li>Terms agreement record (timestamp and version)</li>
    <li>Lock screen widget preference</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>4. Cloud Backup</h2>
  <p>The App offers an optional cloud backup feature. If you enable cloud backup:</p>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Apple users:</strong> Your data is backed up to your personal Apple iCloud account using Apple&rsquo;s CloudKit framework. The backup is stored in your iCloud account, which is controlled by you and governed by Apple&rsquo;s Privacy Policy.</li>
    <li><strong>Google users:</strong> Your data is backed up to a private app folder in your personal Google Drive account. This folder is not visible in your regular Google Drive interface. It is stored in your Google account, which is controlled by you and governed by Google&rsquo;s Privacy Policy.</li>
  </ul>
  <p>Task View LLC does not have access to your iCloud or Google Drive data. We do not store copies of your backup on our servers. We have no servers that receive or process your personal data.</p>
  <p>Cloud backup is optional. If you do not enable it, your data remains only on your device.</p>

  <h2>5. Authentication</h2>
  <p>If you use Sign in with Apple or Sign in with Google to enable cloud backup, we receive a unique identifier from Apple or Google that allows us to associate your backup with your account. We do not receive or store your Apple ID email address or Google account email address on our servers. This identifier is stored locally on your device only.</p>

  <h2>8. Data Security</h2>
  <p>Because your data is stored on your device and in your own cloud accounts, its security is primarily governed by the security of your device and your Apple or Google account. We encourage you to use a strong device passcode and to keep your device&rsquo;s operating system up to date.</p>
  <p>Task View LLC does not maintain servers that store your personal data and therefore cannot be responsible for server-side data breaches involving your task data.</p>
  <p>The App stores your data in a locally encrypted database on your device. The encryption key is stored in your device&rsquo;s secure keychain (iOS Keychain on Apple devices, Android Keystore on Android devices) and is never transmitted off your device.</p>

  <h2>9. Data Retention and Deletion</h2>
  <p>Your data is retained on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App. The App provides a Delete All Data option in Settings that permanently removes all data from your device. If you have cloud backup enabled, you may delete your backup data through your iCloud or Google Drive settings.</p>
  <p>We do not retain copies of your data after you delete it from your device and cloud accounts.</p>
