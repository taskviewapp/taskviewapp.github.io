Privacy Policy

Task View LLC  •  Effective May 2026  •  Version 1.1

The short version: Task View is designed with privacy at its core. Your data is stored on your device. We do not collect, store, or have access to your personal task data. We do not sell your data. We do not use your data for advertising.

1. Introduction

Task View LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates the Task View personal organizer application (“the App”). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in connection with your use of the App.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

Task View LLC does not collect, store, or have access to:

  • Your task lists, to-do items, or notes
  • Your Brain Dump entries
  • Your weekly or daily planning data
  • Your search history within the App
  • Your location
  • Your contacts
  • Your device identifiers beyond what Apple and Google provide to all developers through their platforms

Regulated and Protected Data

Task View is not designed, audited, or certified for use with regulated or protected data categories. We do not knowingly process, and you may not use the App to store or manage:

  • Protected health information (PHI) as defined under HIPAA
  • Financial account numbers, payment card information, or banking credentials
  • Social Security numbers or government-issued identification numbers
  • Any data subject to PCI-DSS, GLBA, SOX, FERPA, or similar regulatory frameworks
  • Any other category of data subject to specific legal protection or professional confidentiality obligations

Users are solely responsible for ensuring that any content entered into the App complies with applicable laws and regulations. Task View LLC expressly disclaims all liability arising from the use of the App with regulated or protected data.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

All data you create in the App — including tasks, notes, priorities, Brain Dump entries, and settings — is stored locally on your device using SQLite, a local encrypted database. This data never leaves your device except as described in Section 4 (Cloud Backup) and Section 6 (Third-Party Services).

The following settings are stored locally on your device:

  • Display name (first name used for personalized greetings)
  • Week start day preference
  • Date format preference
  • Terms agreement record (timestamp and version)
  • Lock screen widget preference

4. Cloud Backup

The App offers an optional cloud backup feature. If you enable cloud backup:

  • Apple users: Your data is backed up to your personal Apple iCloud account using Apple’s CloudKit framework, governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.
  • Google users: Your data is backed up to a private app folder in your personal Google Drive account, governed by Google’s Privacy Policy.

Task View LLC does not have access to your iCloud or Google Drive data. We do not store copies of your backup on our servers. Cloud backup is optional.

5. Authentication

If you use Sign in with Apple or Sign in with Google to enable cloud backup, we receive a unique identifier that allows us to associate your backup with your account. We do not receive or store your Apple ID or Google account email address on our servers.

6. Third-Party Services

6.1 Apple App Store / Google Play Store

Your subscription is managed through the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. Payment information is handled entirely by Apple or Google. Task View LLC does not receive or store your payment information.

6.2 RevenueCat

The App uses RevenueCat to manage subscription status and validate purchases. RevenueCat may collect limited technical information such as a device identifier and subscription state. RevenueCat does not receive your personal task data. RevenueCat Privacy Policy

6.3 Sentry

The App uses Sentry for crash reporting. If the App crashes, Sentry may collect limited technical information including device type, OS version, app version, and a stack trace. Sentry does not receive your personal task data. Sentry Privacy Policy

6.4 Apple and Google Platform Services

As with all apps distributed through the App Store and Google Play, Apple and Google may collect certain technical and usage information through their platforms.

7. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us at admin@taskviewapp.com.

8. Data Security

Your data is stored in a locally encrypted database on your device. The encryption key is stored in your device’s secure keychain and is never transmitted off your device. Task View LLC does not maintain servers that store your personal data.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is retained on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App. The App provides a Delete All Data option in Settings that permanently removes all data from your device. We do not retain copies of your data after deletion.

10. Your Rights

Because Task View LLC does not store your personal task data, you exercise your data rights directly through your device settings and your Apple or Google account settings.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by updating the Effective Date and, where appropriate, by providing notice within the App.

12. California Privacy Rights

Because Task View LLC does not sell personal information and does not collect personal task data on its servers, many CCPA provisions may not apply. For questions, please contact us at the address below.
