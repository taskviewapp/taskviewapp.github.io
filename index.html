Why Task View

Most productivity apps are built for people who love systems. Task View is built for everyone else. It gives you just enough structure to capture your day, see your week, and never lose track of anything — without the cognitive overhead of learning a complex tool.

  • Nothing falls through the cracks — missed tasks resurface automatically in your Inbox
  • Permanent searchable record — everything you’ve ever captured is always retrievable
  • Past days are locked — your history is preserved exactly as it was
  • Your data stays on your device — Task View never transmits anything to any server
  • No required setup — open the app and start planning immediately
  • No gamification — no Karma scores, no streaks, no reward loops

Screens

Features

Day Screen

Top Priorities and To Do in one clean card. Tap to edit inline, swipe to delete, long press for recurring. Past days locked read-only.

Week View

See your entire week at a glance. Each day gets its own card. Only actionable days shown.

Brain Dump

Permanent capture space for everything your brain needs to offload. Timestamped and searchable forever. Nothing you put here ever disappears.

Search

Live full-text search across every screen — tasks, notes, and Brain Dump entries. Your entire history is always one search away.

Inbox

Uncompleted tasks resurface automatically. Mark done, move to today, or schedule for a future date. Nothing is silently dropped.

Recurring Tasks

Daily, weekly, monthly, or custom days. Choose your own duration. Manage, extend, or stop recurrences at any time.

Biometric Lock

Protect your data with Face ID or Touch ID. Your planning history stays private.

Data Export

Export your complete data at any time as CSV files. Your data is always yours to keep.

9 Themes

Classic, Dark, Mint, and more. Find the visual environment that helps you focus.

Privacy

Task View is built on a simple principle: your data belongs to you. Your tasks, notes, and planning history never leave your device. Task View has no server, no account, no sync, and no analytics. There is nothing to breach because there is nothing stored anywhere but your device.
